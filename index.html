About ArcTech

Our Story

Founded in 2020, ArcTech started with a simple mission: make premium tech accessible while reducing electronic waste. What began as a small refurbishment operation has grown into a trusted source for certified refurbished electronics.

Our team of certified technicians inspects every device through a rigorous 50-point quality check. We believe technology should be sustainable without compromising on performance or reliability.

Our Values

  • Sustainability First: Every refurbished device saves resources and reduces e-waste
  • Quality Guaranteed: 2-year warranty on all products, no exceptions
  • Customer Focused: Free shipping and hassle-free returns
  • Transparency: Clear grading system so you know exactly what you're getting

The Team

We're a diverse team of tech enthusiasts, engineers, and sustainability advocates based in Vancouver, BC. Our passion for technology and the environment drives everything we do.
